What's different in this Discord

Nothing about how you chat, call or browse has been redesigned. Every change sits behind the scenes — this version stops reporting on you, stops trying to sell you things, and doesn't freeze in calls.

What you'll actually notice

  • Gone
    No ads, no Nitro popups, no "get more" buttons

    Tapping where they used to be does nothing, instead of opening a sales page.

  • Gone
    No Spotify

    The integration is removed on purpose — see the note below before reporting it.

  • Gone
    The Shop and Quest areas have disappeared

    Including the quest panel that used to overlay the app, and the guild shop row in the sidebar.

  • Better
    Calls are more stable

    The specific bug where a call dropped but the notification still said you were connected is fixed.

  • Better
    Lighter on the phone

    Less happening in the background means better battery life and fewer stutters.

It stops reporting what you do

Discord normally sends a steady stream of information about how you use the app — which screens you open, which buttons you press, how long sessions last, how well your device is performing. All of it is switched off.

  • Off
    Usage tracking

    Roughly a hundred separate places in the app that used to report activity now do nothing at all.

  • Off
    The crash reporter, at every level

    Sentry is disabled three times over: the part built into the app, the part inside the JavaScript engine, and the part that would normally start by itself at launch.

  • Off
    Your user ID in the phone's system log

    During a call the app used to write your ID — and everyone else's — into the system log every single second. It doesn't any more.

  • Off
    Telling Discord the app is modified

    The app no longer flags itself as altered when it connects.

Advertising and attribution

These are the services apps use to work out who installed them and how to target advertising. Every one is disabled, both in the app and at the network level.

  • Blocked
    AppsFlyer

    Including its short-link domain, used for tracking which link an install came from.

  • Blocked
    Google Analytics and Firebase Analytics

    Firebase's messaging service is deliberately left working — that's what delivers your push notifications.

  • Removed
    Google's advertising ID

    The permission is stripped out of the app entirely, so it can't ask for it even if it wanted to.

  • Blocked
    The rest

    Adjust · Amplitude · Segment · Braintree's analytics · Google's mobile-ads beacon · PayPal's conversion tracking · the Play Store install referrer.

Shops, quests and Nitro

Everything that exists to take payment or drive engagement is blocked or hidden. Nothing is broken by this — if you already have Nitro, it still works. The app just stops advertising it to you.

  • Blocked
    The Shop

    Every storefront, price, catalogue and purchase request is refused before it leaves the phone.

  • Hidden
    Quests

    The panel that overlaid the app is hidden and quest data is never requested.

  • Hidden
    Nitro popups, banners and gift buttons

    The two internal entry points every sales prompt goes through are switched off, so none of them open.

  • Blocked
    Guild boosts, gifts, wishlists, virtual currency, role subscriptions, referrals and promotions

    All refused at the network.

Spotify — this one is on purpose

The Spotify integration is switched off: connecting an account, "Listen Along", the Spotify card on your profile, and the app announcing what you're listening to. This was a deliberate decision when the build was made. Please don't report it as something that needs fixing.

Privacy

  • Off
    Your contacts are no longer read

    The app used to read your address book to suggest friends. It now returns an empty list instead.

  • Off
    It no longer checks what else you have installed

    Discord used to probe your phone for around twenty different social and gaming apps.

  • Closed
    The game-integration service is no longer exposed

    It used to be reachable by any other app on your phone, with no permission check at all.

The freeze fixes

The original problem was a call that froze the phone and then dropped, while the notification still claimed you were connected. Several separate causes turned up.

  • Fixed
    Calls dropping while still showing "connected"

    When you put the app in the background mid-call, Android was demoting the call from a microphone task to a background data task — which silently cuts your microphone. The app now keeps claiming the microphone. This is the main fix.

  • Fixed
    The app was making its own freezes worse

    When it noticed its JavaScript was struggling, it responded by taking a full performance profile of the struggling code, writing it to disk and reading it back — more work, at the worst moment. That's gone.

  • Fixed
    A timer that ran forever

    Every video tile had a timer firing every four seconds, doing work whose result was thrown away.

  • Fixed
    Every network request was doing unnecessary work

    The app was assembling a copy of all its own request headers — including one that's several kilobytes long — then searching that text 81 times. On every single request. It now looks only at the web address.

What has not changed

  • Messaging, voice, video, notifications and file uploads all work normally.
  • Push notifications work. An earlier version broke them; that was fixed.
  • Wishlist and profile decorations were not hidden. Those screens still work as normal — that piece wasn't finished.
  • A few Nitro elements remain inside the app's compiled code — some emoji picker decorations. They can't be removed without editing compiled binaries.
  • You can't buy anything. That's a consequence, not a bug. If you need to make a purchase, use the official Discord app.
  • You are not anonymous. This build stops the extra reporting; Discord can still identify you from your account, your IP address and normal usage.

Worth checking after your next call

  1. Start a call, then press home. Wait a minute and come back. If the call is still connected, the main fix is working.
  2. Check audio and video still behave normally in a call.

If you hit a problem, note what you were doing at the time and pass it on.
